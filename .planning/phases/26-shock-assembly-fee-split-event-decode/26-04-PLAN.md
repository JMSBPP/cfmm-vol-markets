<the_control_is_what_makes_it_a_differential>
`volume_path.gms` aborts with **exit 3** for `abort$`, for `execerror`, and for every named abort
alike (24-RESEARCH guard 15), and section 4 forbids reading the log to decide a verdict. So "the
boundary row aborted" does NOT by itself say the ELLIPSE gate is what rejected it: the kappa range
check (`kappa in [1e-12, 1e12]`) and CONOPT's own `Locally Infeasible` (section 1.4, volume too
small for `delta*`) abort identically and are NOT fee-pair properties.

Therefore **every fee pair in the grid carries a CONTROL invocation** — the same pair at a
comfortably interior `delta*` — and a row whose `control_exit /= 0` is an UNATTRIBUTED abort and
fails the check. This is `26-RESEARCH.md` guard 17 and Open Question 5, and it is the one thing the
capture will actually discover.

**If a control aborts**, raise `volTgtWad` for that pair until it solves, RECORD the value used in
the artifact, and state it in the summary. Never silently accept an unattributed abort, and never
drop the row.
</the_control_is_what_makes_it_a_differential>
