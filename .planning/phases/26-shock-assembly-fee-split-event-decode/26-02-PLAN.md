<the_zero_trap>
This is the plan's headline hazard and it is NOT hypothetical.

`ShockLib.shock_emit` calls `@evm_log2(buf, 96, SHOCK_EVENT_TOPIC0, pool)` — exactly two topics and
exactly three 32-byte data words, in order `[tickDiff, txlVolmNormRate, txlVolmDecay]`.

Issue #28 guarantees that in v6.0 scope the on-chain hookData is tagged `flags = 0b010`, so
**`tickDiff == 0` and `txlVolmDecay == 0` on every production log, by construction**. `Shock.plk`'s
accessors return a literal `0` when their flag bit is unset, and `shock_decode` ACCEPTS `flags = 0`
(it requires `buf.length == 1 + 3k` and `k = 0` with length 1 passes) — so an all-zero 96-byte
payload is **legally emittable upstream**, with a provenance, not merely the shape an absent subject
takes.

**Two of three data words are always zero in production, and `tickDiff` is the sign-extended one.**
A decoder that never sign-extends, that reads the wrong word, or that defaults to zero on failure is
GREEN on every real log. This is the `tickSpacing = 0` finding (`Main.hs:1534`) arriving
pre-guaranteed by the emitter.

Consequences, non-negotiable:
- the synthetic corpus MUST carry a **negative** `tickDiff` and a **nonzero** `txlVolmDecay`, which
  production never emits;
- an all-zero payload MUST be REFUSED by name, never decoded into a shock of zeroes;
- the corpus's discriminating property is asserted as a SET over MEMBER NAMES in both directions —
  phase 24 MEASURED a count-preserving rename leaving a count-based assertion green.

`shock_tick_diff` ends in `@evm_signextend(2, raw)`, so the word on the wire is sign-extended to the
full 256 bits and `RealizedVol.Decode.signed_word` is EXACTLY the right conversion. **A 24-bit mask
is WRONG.** `shock_txl_volm_norm_rate` and `shock_txl_volm_decay` end in `& MASK_U24` — unsigned,
range `[0, 2^24)` — and applying `signed_word` to them would be a SILENT bug, since a u24 never
reaches 2^255.
</the_zero_trap>
