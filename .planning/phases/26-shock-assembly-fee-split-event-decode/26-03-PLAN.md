<ordering_is_load_bearing>
The ninth refusal runs **AFTER** the seven `in_range` refusals and **AFTER** `distinct_fees`.

Reason, MEASURED: `min_admissible_dstar 3000 3000 == Nothing` — the ellipse test refuses `x == m`

refused with an ellipse message and `VOLUME_PATH.md` section 1.2's specific diagnosis would be

why `equal_fees_are_refused_in_haskell_with_the_1_2_diagnosis` asserts the MESSAGE names section 1.2
AND the CONSTRUCTOR, rather than asserting that a refusal happened.

**This ordering must survive 26-04's refactor, and the thing that survives is the BEHAVIOUR, not a
line number.** 26-04 splits this function into `render_argv_ungated` (the eight pre-existing
refusals) and `render_argv` (nine). After that split, `distinct_fees` and `admissible_pair` live in
DIFFERENT functions and no line-number comparison between them means anything. The composition
26-04 is required to write is

```haskell

  _    <- admissible_pair shock       -- the ninth runs only after all eight passed
```

forbidden. The durable gate across both plans is check 14's constructor arm: an equal-fee shock must
