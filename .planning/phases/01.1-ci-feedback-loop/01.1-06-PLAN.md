## The push build is deliberately powerless

No `environment:`, no approval, and **no secrets** — including no `API_KEY`. It runs unattended on
the self-hosted runner, so it gets the minimum: the suite runs `--offline` with no mainnet-fork
test, and `${...}` in `[rpc_endpoints]` is measured to resolve at alias *use*, not at config load.
It cannot approve a merge and cannot reach anything the gate can.

## Why the pin is not `foundry-rs/foundry-toolchain@v1`

That action installs into `$HOME/.foundry` (`src/index.ts`:
`FOUNDRY_DIR = path.join(os.homedir(), ".foundry")`). On a persistent self-hosted runner that is
the box's shared foundry directory, so the action would have an unattended workflow rewrite the
machine's default `forge` on every push — the exact drift the pin exists to stop. It also requires
`node24` on the runner agent. Instead both workflows drive the same pinned `foundryup` installer
into a pin-keyed `$HOME/.foundry-pins/$VERSION` and prepend it to the job's `PATH`: nothing outside
the job is mutated, and the install is paid once per pin.
