`publish_bytes`, in this order, and the order is the design:

1. `decode_artifact` the bytes; a `Left` is `ArtifactUnparseable`. That is the shape floor's hard
   half and it is the DECODER's rather than a second transcription: `post_conditions` already
   refuses `nEvents <= 0` and `length dQx /= nEvents`, which is the same equality the consuming
   forge test asserts at `assertEq(dQx.length, nEvents)`.
2. `BS.length bytes >= fixture_min_bytes`, else `BelowShapeFloor`. The floor is non-vacuous by
   construction and a check asserts `fixture_min_bytes < volume_path_golden_bytes_len` FIRST — a
   floor above every real artifact refuses everything and passes its own refusal table.
3. The pool token: `render_address_token (fi_pool identity)` must be exactly
   `2 + address_hex_digits` characters, and must not equal `zero_address_token`. TWO separate arms,
   because the zero address is SHAPE-VALID — 27-03 MEASURED that folding them leaves the zero arm
   unreachable.
4. The splice, TEXTUAL. Emit `{`, then the three `fixture_identity_entries` as `"key":value` joined
   by commas, then a comma, then EVERY BYTE of the artifact after its own first `{`. If the
   artifact's first non-whitespace byte is not `{`, refuse `NotAJsonObject`. **Do not decode and
   re-render.** `dQx[0]` is `-2613128317657530400`, above the 53-bit ceiling, and BYTE-04 measured
   that class of value moving by 32 wei through a carrier that re-renders it: the stored bytes are
   the oracle, and publication must not quietly become a second renderer of them.

`publish_fixture dir identity bytes` calls `publish_bytes`, and — **only if the directory already
exists** — writes `dir </> fixture_file_name` through `write_bytes_atomically`. It NEVER creates the
directory; 28-04 owns that failure and its text.
