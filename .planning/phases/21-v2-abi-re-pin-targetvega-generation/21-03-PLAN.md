<objective>
Rewrite `decode_order_created` for E1 v2 and prove the fourth field is genuinely COMPARED on
readback, not merely carried.

Purpose: RPIN-04 is NOT a constant swap. The emitter changed from `@evm_log3`/160 bytes to
`@evm_log2`/128 bytes, so the shipped decoder — which pattern-matches a THREE-element topic list
and reads data words 2/3/4 — returns `Nothing` on every v2 log and reports it forever as "unknown".
Swapping the topic0 alone leaves that failure fully intact. Separately, `target_vega` joins
`VolOrder`'s derived `Eq`, which makes `verify_mined_order` compare it for free — a change
invisible in a diff, and therefore one that needs an observed RED as its only real evidence.

Output: a V2 `OrderCreatedEvent` and decoder, a rewritten `report_order_created`, and checks that
positively decode a v2 log, reject the v1 shape and both retired topic0s, and demonstrate the
targetVega readback failure.
</objective>
