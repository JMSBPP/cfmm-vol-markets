**Docblock — write these facts in-file** (a future reader must not re-litigate them):
- WHY tolerance 0 is GUARANTEED, not hoped, within int24 x uint32: the operator trees match exactly
  (`k**2 * SS` == `(k*%k)*%SS`; `6*b*k*seq` is left-assoc on both sides; `6*dt*b**2` == `(6*%dt)*%(b*%b)`
  since Solidity `**` binds tighter than `*`); |b| <= 2^56 so the numerator peaks ~2^149 << 2^256 —
  NEITHER side wraps, so they cannot wrap DIFFERENTLY; and `@evm_sdiv` IS the SDIV opcode Solidity's
  `int256 /` compiles to under `unchecked` (identical truncation toward zero).
- WHY tolerance 0 is REGIME-CONDITIONAL: it is NOT claimed in Algebra's deliberate int56-overflow
  regime (Plank's full-width in-flight accumulator does not replicate the `int56` wrap —
  `RealizedVolatilityLib.plk` vs `VolatilityOracle.sol:357`). The int24/uint32 bounds above keep this
  corpus out of that regime.
- WHY dt is bounded `>= 1`: dt=0 is a KNOWN, EXCLUDED divergence — Solidity `/` reverts with Panic
  0x12 even inside `unchecked`, while EVM `SDIV(N,0)` returns 0 silently. Unbounded, the fuzzer draws
  0 and the mock reverts, failing the run for the WRONG reason.
- WHY the full uint256 and not uint88: production truncates to uint88, but comparing the whole
  returned word is strictly stronger on a FREE axis — it catches high-bit divergence a mutant could
  introduce.
- WHAT THIS KILLS (fill in after Task 2 with the OBSERVED results): the harness argument-order swap,
  and the kernel middle-term coefficient `6 -> 7`.
- WHAT THIS DOES NOT REPLACE: the probe's independent anchor `819430`. A purely differential
  assertion would be satisfied by a mock that merely ECHOED Plank; the probe pins Algebra to an
  externally-derived value. Both are needed.
