<objective>
Execute the bulk accumulator read: roughly 8,000-15,000 archive `eth_call`s against
`SFPM.getAccountPremium`, checkpointed to disk row by row.

Everything up to this point is pure and offline-tested. This plan is the one that spends 30-60
minutes of network time, and its single design requirement follows from that: **a run that only
writes at the end is a run you do twice.** Phase 9 learned this on the 3-hour swap-log pull;
`Panel.Variance` streams each chunk to disk as it arrives, and this driver reuses that discipline.

The second requirement is fail-loud. A public archive endpoint that degrades to "state not
available" and returns `0x` must abort the run, not decode to zero — a zero accumulator is
indistinguishable from a real zero and would pass silently through the reconciliation gate as a
position that "earned nothing".

Purpose: materialise the raw data the gate and the panel are both built from, once, correctly, and
resumably.
Output: `premium-accumulators.csv` covering the full read schedule.
</objective>

- CSV schema (with a `#` provenance banner: date, RPC URL, SFPM address, vegoid, schedule size,
  git commit):
  `token_type,tick_lower,tick_upper,block,is_long,at_tick,acc0_x64,acc1_x64,net_liquidity,removed_liquidity,epoch,endpoint`
  Accumulators and liquidity are written as **decimal `Integer` strings**, never floats — these
  exceed 2^63 and a `Double` round-trip would silently lose precision.
- `runReadSchedule primaryEnv failoverEnv csvPath schedule`:
  - `loadAccumulators csvPath`, then `pendingRows` — **resumption is the default path**, not a flag.
  - For each pending row: `getAccountPremium` at `BlockNumber rrBlock` with `rrAtTick`, then
    `getAccountLiquidity` at the same block (RESEARCH Pitfall 5: liquidity is what disambiguates
    "chunk empty" from "no fees"; do not skip it to halve the call count).
  - **Append the row to disk immediately** after each successful pair. Flush the handle; do not
    rely on process-exit buffering.
  - Progress to stderr every 100 rows: `[read n/m] calls=... elapsed=...s rate=.../s`.
  - On a `Left` from the primary endpoint after `Chain.Rpc`'s retries are exhausted, retry that row
    once on `failoverEnv` (`base.drpc.org`) and count it in `rsFailoverCalls`. If the failover also
    fails, **return `Left` naming the exact key** and stop. Do NOT write a zero, do NOT skip the
    row, do NOT continue — a hole in the accumulator series becomes a silent wrong premium, which is
    precisely the class of failure Phase 9 died of.
  - Count into `rsFlagged` any row where `net_liquidity == 0` (`ChunkEmpty`) or either accumulator
    trips `Panoptic.Premium.isFrozenAcc` (`AccFrozen`), and report both counts at the end.
