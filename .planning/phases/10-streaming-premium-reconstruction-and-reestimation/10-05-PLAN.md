<objective>
Implement the premium read itself: encode the `getAccountPremium` call, decode its packed return, and
turn accumulator differences into per-position, per-epoch premium in ETH wei.

This is the heart of the amended route. The identity being evaluated is
`premium = (acc(t_end) - acc(t_start)) * legLiquidity / 2^64`, with the accumulator read from the
contract that defines it — so correctness here is a matter of encoding, scaling, and sign, not of
modelling. Three specific traps get explicit handling: `bytes poolKey` is a **dynamic** ABI argument
(its head slot is an offset, not data); accumulator subtraction is **unchecked** and must wrap mod
2^128; and `getAccountPremium` **silently falls back** to the stored accumulator when a chunk's
netLiquidity is zero, which is indistinguishable from "no fees" unless liquidity is recorded
alongside.

Purpose: every number in the panel and the gate flows through these two modules. They are small,
pure, and fully testable offline against frozen live data — so they should be exactly right before
15,000 network calls are spent on them.
Output: `Panoptic.Sfpm`, `Panoptic.Premium`, and their offline specs including the telescoping proof.
</objective>

- `accDelta hi lo = Chain.Abi.diffMod 128 hi lo`. **Never** a bare `hi - lo`: the accumulators are
  `uint128` under Solidity `unchecked` semantics, and a naive `Integer` subtraction returns a huge
  negative number instead of the intended small positive one whenever the value has wrapped
  (RESEARCH Pitfall 3). Add the comment.
- `premiumWei accHi accLo legLiquidity isLong`, per RESEARCH "Code Examples" and
  `PanopticPool._getPremia` L2296-2298:
  ```haskell
  premiumWei accHi accLo legLiquidity isLong =
    let d = accDelta accHi accLo
        p = (d * legLiquidity) `div` (2 ^ (64 :: Int))
    in if isLong then negate p else p
  ```
  The `2^64` is the X64 accumulator scale — NOT `2^128` (that is `feeGrowth`) and NOT `2^96` (that
  is `sqrtPrice`). RESEARCH Pitfall 2 lists "off by exactly 2^64, 2^128, 1e12" as the diagnostic
  signature of getting this wrong. Result units are **token wei**; the `/1e18` (ETH) or `/1e6`
  (USDC) conversion happens strictly downstream of the gate.
- `telescope readings legLiquidity isLong` sums consecutive `premiumWei` deltas. Its spec assertion
  is EXACT equality with the endpoint `premiumWei`, because the reconciliation gate's entire
  legitimacy is that the panel is a *decomposition* of the ground truth rather than an independent
  estimate of it.
- `isFrozenAcc a = a >= (2^128 - 1) * 99 `div` 100`. RESEARCH Pitfall 6:
  `LeftRightLibrary.addCapped` freezes owed and gross together once either hits `2^128 - 1`, after
  which every delta is 0 — indistinguishable from "no fees" unless the LEVEL is checked. Any
  observation whose reading trips this must carry `AccFrozen`.
- `PremiumFlag`: `ChunkEmpty` when `arNetLiquidity == 0` (the `atTick` extrapolation silently fell
  back to the stored value — RESEARCH Pitfall 5), `AccFrozen` per above, `Extrapolated` when the
  reading used a real `atTick` rather than the sentinel. Flags must be carried through to the panel
  CSV so the gate and the analysis can see them; a flagged observation is not automatically dropped,
  but it must never be invisible.
- `multiplierWedge removedLiq netLiq isLong` returns the Panoptic multiplier as a `Rational`:
  `1 + nu*R/N` for long legs, `1 + nu*R^2/(N*T)` for short legs, with `nu = 1/8`, `T = N + R`.
  This is NOT used to compute the premium (the contract already applies it inside the accumulator) —
  it exists solely so plan 10-11 can report the MEASURED distribution of the Lean-vs-Panoptic wedge
  in `lean-haskell-crosswalk.md` instead of asserting a bound. It equals exactly 1 when `R = 0` and
  is bounded above by `1 + nu = 1.125` on the long side.
- `buildPremiumObs` fans the deduplicated chunk-level readings back out to per-(tokenId, leg, epoch)
  observations by multiplying each chunk delta by that leg's `lcLiquidity`.
