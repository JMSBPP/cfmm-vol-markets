<objective>
Build the reconciliation machinery and prove it on five spells before spending the full gate.

The gate's legitimacy rests on a structural argument: `OptionBurn.premiaByLeg` is, by construction,
`(acc(t_burn) - s_options[mint]) * legLiquidity / 2^64`, and the per-epoch panel sums
**telescopically** over the same accumulator to the same endpoint difference. The reconstructed
panel is therefore a *decomposition* of the ground truth, not an independent estimate of it — which
is exactly why the tolerance is 1% and not 10%. A miss between 1% and 10% means an unaccounted
wedge, and should trigger diagnosis rather than celebration.

RESEARCH prescribes running a 5-spell pre-check first: if the error is not already below 1% on five
short single-leg spells, something is structurally wrong and running 61 will only produce 61 wrong
numbers. This plan builds the machinery and executes that pre-check. Plan 10-08 runs the real gate.

Purpose: separate "is the reconstruction right?" from "does the whole sample pass?", so a failure is
locatable.
Output: `Panel.Reconcile`, its offline spec, the `reconcile` CLI, and a 5-spell pre-check report.
</objective>
