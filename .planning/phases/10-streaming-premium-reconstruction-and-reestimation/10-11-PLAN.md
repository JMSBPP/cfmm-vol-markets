Close the phase out honestly: record the wedge between what Lean models and what was actually
estimated, and update the project's roadmap and state.

Phase 10's premium is **Panoptic's** premium — fee growth times the utilization-based multiplier
(`nu = 1/VEGOID = 1/8`). Lean's `Panoptic.streamingPremium` and the project's own
`STREAMING_PREMIUM.md` model the **bare** LP fee-revenue identity, without that multiplier. The two
objects differ by a factor between 1 (when no long interest exists in the chunk) and 1.125.

a documented wedge between the Lean object and the estimated object — the cross-walk table must
record it rather than paper over it." The formal witness claim rests on the fidelity of the
Lean-to-Haskell correspondence, so an undocumented wedge would quietly weaken the very claim the
cross-walk exists to support.

Purpose: keep the auditable fidelity record accurate, including where it is imperfect.
