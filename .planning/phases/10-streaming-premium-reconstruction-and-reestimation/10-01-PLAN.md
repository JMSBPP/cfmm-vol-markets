Measure — before ANY reconstruction work is planned or run — whether this market can supply a
position-epoch panel materially larger than Phase 9's 61 accrual spells.

`PanopticPool._getPremia` (`PanopticPool.sol` L2250) **skips every leg with `width == 0`**: such
legs accrue no premium at all. Research sampled the subgraph and found the first `Leg` records all
carried `width: 0`. The phase's entire premise — a ~x100 sample gain (~55 positions x ~119 epochs
versus 61 spells) — is therefore **UNVERIFIED**. If the usable panel is not materially larger than
61 observations, the phase cannot deliver its power goal and must STOP AND REPORT rather than spend
six days building a reconstruction pipeline that cannot help.

Purpose: convert the phase's load-bearing assumption into a measurement, and put the STOP/GO
decision in the user's hands.
Output: a census CSV, an audit report with an explicit recommendation, a frozen subgraph fixture,
and a user decision.
