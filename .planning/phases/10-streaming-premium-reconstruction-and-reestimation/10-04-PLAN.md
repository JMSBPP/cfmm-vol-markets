<objective>
Turn subgraph `Leg` records into the exact chunk coordinates and liquidity amounts that
`SFPM.getAccountPremium` and the premium identity require.

Two quantities are needed per leg, and both have a specific, non-obvious definition in Panoptic's
source:
1. **The chunk key** `(tokenType, tickLower, tickUpper)` from `PanopticMath.getTicks` — with a
   **floor-down / ceil-up asymmetry** that a "symmetric +/- half-width" simplification silently
   breaks for odd `width * tickSpacing`.
2. **The leg liquidity** `L` from `PanopticMath.getLiquidityChunk` — the multiplier in
   `premium = deltaAcc_X64 * L / 2^64`. Get `L` wrong and every premium is wrong by a constant
   factor per position, which the reconciliation gate would surface as a large, position-specific
   error.

RESEARCH is explicit that the subgraph's own `Chunk.tickLower/tickUpper` is the authority and the
formula is the **cross-check**, not the other way round.

Purpose: fix the geometry before any accumulator is read, so a gate failure in 10-08 cannot be
blamed on tick arithmetic.
Output: `Panoptic.Chunk` plus an offline spec that validates `getTicks` against every chunk in the
frozen fixture.
</objective>
