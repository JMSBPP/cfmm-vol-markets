<objective>
Re-run the **unchanged** Phase-9 estimator on the gate-validated position-epoch panel, and adjudicate
the pre-committed stopping rule.

Everything about the estimator stays exactly as Phase 9 built and tested it: GSL Levenberg-Marquardt
NLS with the data-scaled multi-start, tokenId-clustered CR0 sandwich standard errors, the three
committed specification tests, the two-noisy-measures EIV instrument, and the four locked
alternatives. **Only the LHS changed.** That is the whole experimental design: if the estimate is now
informative, the difference is attributable to the measurement fix and nothing else.

The success criterion was fixed in advance and is deliberately blind to the answer:

> **Success = an upsilon-0 clustered-CI half-width of at most ~6.2e-5** (one quarter of Phase 9's
> +/-2.48e-4) — **regardless of kappa-hat's sign or significance.**

If the interval is informative, report it, whichever direction it points. If it is not, the phase
reports that this market cannot identify upsilon and stops. No respecification, no subsample
hunting, no estimator fishing. The `anti-fishing-replication` discipline is the tripwire: if anyone,
human or agent, proposes moving this bar mid-run, that proposal is itself the finding.

Purpose: obtain an honest answer under a rule written before the answer was known.
Output: the estimation panel export, the self-describing analysis document, and a user adjudication.
</objective>
