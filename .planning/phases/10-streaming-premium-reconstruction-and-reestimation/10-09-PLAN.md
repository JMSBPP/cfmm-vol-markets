<objective>
Restore the approved spec's **position-epoch panel** — the unit of observation Phase 9 could not
construct — and join it to the existing variance series.

Spec section 1 asks for one observation per Panoptic tokenId per daily epoch, because that is what
identifies the model: *within-position* time variation in variance identifies the vega level, and
*cross-position* strike variation identifies the moneyness profile. Phase 9 was forced down to the
accrual spell (one row per mint-burn pair) because the subgraph carried no per-epoch premium series,
which collapsed the within-position dimension entirely and left `kappa` structurally unidentified.

The gate-validated accumulator series restores that dimension. This plan does **only** the LHS
reconstruction: `Panel.Build.dailyEpoch` stays byte-identical, `Panel.Variance` is untouched, and
the estimator is not modified at all.

Purpose: hand the unchanged Phase-9 estimator the panel the spec always asked for.
Output: `panel-epoch.csv` joined cleanly to `variance.csv`, plus the DATA-SOURCES update recording
the restored unit of observation.
</objective>
