<objective>
**OPTIONAL, NON-BLOCKING.** Independently reimplement Panoptic's `_getPremiaDeltas` in Haskell and
check it against the on-chain accumulator, honouring the spirit of the original (withdrawn)
full-replay decision at a small fraction of its cost.

The original locked decision was a full Uniswap V4 fee replay, on the reasoning that re-deriving the
identity independently would prove the reconstruction correct. That decision was withdrawn because
its input does not exist (the cached swap stream is a two-column `timestamp_unix,tick` file) and
because exact replay from events alone is impossible — `feeGrowthGlobal` updates per swap *step*
with a step-varying liquidity divisor that the `Swap` event does not expose. RESEARCH costed the
full route at 3-4 weeks and noted it would still need the archive reads as its own correctness
oracle.

This plan preserves the *verification value* of that decision cheaply: port the ~15 lines of
`_getPremiaDeltas` arithmetic and confirm they reproduce what the contract returned. It is a
second, independent implementation of the multiplier — the part most likely to harbour a
misunderstanding — without the 256-bit swap-math project.

**This plan is explicitly non-blocking.** The phase succeeds or fails on plans 10-01 through 10-11.
Skip it if time is short; record the skip and move on.

Purpose: an independent check on the one piece of arithmetic the phase takes entirely on faith.
Output: a cross-check module, its offline spec, and a short findings note.

<constants>
_getPremiaDeltas (SemiFungiblePositionManagerV4.sol L1129-1214), EXACT Solidity:

  uint256 totalLiquidity   = netLiquidity + removedLiquidity;
  premium0X64_base         = Math.mulDiv(collected0, totalLiquidity * 2**64, netLiquidity ** 2);

  // owed (long / buyer):
  numerator                = netLiquidity + (removedLiquidity / vegoid);
  premium0X64_owed         = mulDiv(premium0X64_base, numerator, totalLiquidity);

  // gross (short / seller):
  numerator                = totalLiquidity**2 - totalLiquidity*removedLiquidity
                             + (removedLiquidity**2 / vegoid);
  premium0X64_gross        = mulDiv(premium0X64_base, numerator, totalLiquidity ** 2);

vegoid = 8. All divisions are Solidity integer division (FLOOR toward zero for non-negative
operands). `removedLiquidity / vegoid` is an INTEGER division applied BEFORE the addition — it is
NOT `(netLiquidity*vegoid + removedLiquidity)/vegoid`. Reproducing the truncation exactly is the
whole point of the exercise.

Results are capped via toUint128Capped at 2^128 - 1 and FROZEN IN PAIRS on overflow
(LeftRightLibrary.addCapped).

Reference graph cited in-source: https://www.desmos.com/calculator/mdeqob2m04

   ```haskell
   module Panoptic.PremiaDeltas
     ( premiumBaseX64, owedNumerator, grossNumerator, premiaDeltas, toUint128Capped ) where
   ```

   - `premiumBaseX64 collected netLiq removedLiq = (collected * (netLiq + removedLiq) * 2^64) `div` (netLiq ^ 2)`
   - `owedNumerator netLiq removedLiq vegoid = netLiq + (removedLiq `div` vegoid)`

     `Chain.Abi.diffMod`, which wraps; the two are used at different points (deltas wrap, stored
     values clamp) and conflating them is a real hazard. Note it in a comment.
