<objective>
Run the hard validation gate on all 61 spells and put its verdict to the user.

This is the phase's central discipline and the thing Phase 9 lacked. Phase 9 produced a null and
only afterwards asked whether the right quantity had been measured. Here that question is answered
**before** any estimator runs: the reconstructed per-epoch premium must sum, over each spell, to the
observed `OptionBurn.premium0` at a median relative error of at most 1%, in ETH wei, with the short
and long strata reported separately.

The gate has three possible honest outcomes, and only one of them leads to estimation:
- **PASS** — the reconstruction is validated; proceed to build the panel.
- **FAIL, diagnosable** — a specific wedge is identified; fix the reconstruction and re-run.
- **FAIL, not diagnosable** — a legitimate terminal phase outcome. Report it.

What is **not** an option is relaxing the tolerance. `gateTolerance` is a single named constant
precisely so that any attempt to move it is a visible diff.

Purpose: convert "did we measure the right thing?" from a post-hoc audit question into a
pre-estimation blocker.
Output: the full gate report, the per-spell error CSV, and a user adjudication.
</objective>
